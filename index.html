About

Hi, I'm Niklas!

I'm a PhD candidate in Software Engineering at the University of Stuttgart, where I'm doing research on LLM-based intelligent tutoring systems, and I'm excited about how generative and agentic AI is changing the way software is built. Before that, I gained several years of professional experience in industry, ranging from a dual study program to roles as an IT Architect and Product Owner within international agile teams, always working at the intersection of customers and development teams. For me, software is the key that enables people and companies to achieve almost anything, and I'm passionate about planning and leading software projects that meet customer needs. In addition to my research, I build websites and e-commerce stores for small and medium-sized businesses. Feel free to reach out or connect!
